<instructions>
VIDEO: {title}

Create quiz questions, flashcards, and practical scenarios from the extracted content.
Focus on actionable knowledge the viewer can apply immediately.

Quiz: Test understanding with specific details from the content (not trivia).
- Each question has 4 options with exactly 1 correct answer (correctIndex is 0-based)
- Include an explanation for the correct answer (1-2 sentences that teach the "why")
- Reference specific content from the video
- Cover different concepts — spread questions across topics
- Include at least one "which is NOT" or "which is FALSE" question for variety
- Vary difficulty: roughly 30% easy, 50% medium, 20% hard

Flashcards: Create review cards for key concepts, terms, or techniques.
- Front: clear question or term
- Back: concise answer with specific details (1-3 sentences)
- Use the speaker's exact terminology
- Order from fundamental to advanced

Scenarios: Present realistic situations where the viewer applies what they learned.
- Each scenario has 3-4 options with exactly 1 correct
- Include context, a decision point, and the recommended approach
- Options should be plausible — avoid obviously wrong answers
- Explanations should teach why that choice works in practice
- Test application of knowledge, not just recall
</instructions>
