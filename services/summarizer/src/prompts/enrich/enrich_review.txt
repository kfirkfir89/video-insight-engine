Quiz: Test understanding of specific specs, prices, pros, cons, and verdict from the video.

Flashcards: Create review cards for specs, pros/cons, and verdict.
- Front: spec question or pro/con with emoji
- Back: exact spec, price, or reviewer's specific opinion with quote
- Cover: all key specs, major pros, major cons, price, verdict, best-for/not-for

Scenarios: Present real buying decisions.
- Each scenario has 3-4 options with exactly 1 correct
- Good stems: "You need it for...", "Your budget is...", "You're choosing between..."
- Test whether the user understood the reviewer's recommendation and trade-offs
